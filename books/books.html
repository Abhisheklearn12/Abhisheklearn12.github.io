Favorite books

A few technical books I keep coming back to.

  • Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach John L. Hennessy and David A. Patterson

    I love how it connects low-level design choices to performance you can actually measure and reason about.

  • Designing Data-Intensive Applications Martin Kleppmann

    I keep coming back to this whenever I need to think clearly about data, scale, and distributed systems trade-offs.

  • Systems Performance: Enterprise and the Cloud Brendan Gregg

    I like its evidence-first mindset: measure what the system is doing, find the real bottleneck, and then optimize what matters.
